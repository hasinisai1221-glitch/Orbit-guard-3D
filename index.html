Scientific Accuracy

Satellite positions are SGP4 predictions calculated from real CelesTrak orbital records, not direct live telemetry. Ground-station visibility is geometric, not measured signal strength. Satellite proximity does not establish collision probability.

Solar results use simplified astronomical geometry, 1361 W/m² solar irradiance and 30% planetary reflectivity. They are not live measurements of Earth's heat absorption.
